DINOv3 pretrained backbone

Download the ViT-H+/16 model pretrained on LVD-1689M from the official DINOv3 project:
https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3#pretrained-models

Follow the official instructions to request access and download the PyTorch checkpoint.
Rename the downloaded file to:
    dinov3_vith16plus.pth

Place it directly in this directory:
    checkpoints/dinov3_vith16plus.pth

This is the local filename used by configs/main.yaml; the original download filename may differ.
The pretrained weights are not included in this repository.
